# Social Media & Space Management Strategy

## 1. Target Handles & Namespaces
To secure the "Slalom Framework" brand identity, the following handles should be claimed:

### Developer Spaces
- **PyPI:** `slalom-framework` or `slalom-streams` (to avoid generic conflicts).
- **GitHub Organization:** `slalom-dev` or `slalom-framework`.
- **Stack Overflow Tag:** `[slalom-python]` or `[slalom-framework]`.

### Social Presence
- **Twitter/X:** `@slalom_dev`
- **LinkedIn:** Create a "Company" page for *Slalom Framework* to list as an open-source project.
- **Instagram/Threads:** `@slalom.framework` (Focus on visual architecture maps and performance graphs).

## 2. Strategy Considerations
- **Name Conflict Management:** If contacted by *Slalom Build*, emphasize the "Framework" suffix and the common-noun nature of the word "Slalom."
- **Community Building:** Focus on "Data Engineering" and "ELT Architecture" communities.
- **Portfolio Linking:** Ensure `slalom.github.io` (or similar) redirects to the personal branding page.

## 3. Brand Protection
- Monitor PyPI for typo-squatting.
- Establish a "Brand Guide" early to ensure third-party adapters use consistent naming (e.g., `slalom-adapter-s3`).
